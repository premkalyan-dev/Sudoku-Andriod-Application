2 Quick Summary (TL;DR)

3 Information We Collect

Skudo does not collect or transmit any personal information to external servers. The app functions entirely locally on your device.

A. Local Gameplay Data (Stored on Your Device Only)

The following game state information is saved strictly within local device storage (Android SQLite Room Database and Jetpack DataStore) so you can resume your games:

  • Puzzles & Statistics: Puzzles completed, win streaks, best solve times, and difficulty records.
  • Player Progression: In-game level, earned coins, and gems.
  • Settings & Customizations: Selected accent color, audio/haptics preferences, and hint counts.
  • Saved Games: Board state, entered digits, pencil marks, and timer of active games.

B. Information We DO NOT Collect

  • No personal identification (name, email address, physical address, phone number).
  • No device advertising IDs (AAID), hardware serial numbers, or IMEI.
  • No precise or approximate geolocation data.
  • No user photos, contacts, camera access, or microphone recordings.

4 How Information is Used

Because no data leaves your device, local information is processed solely by your device's processor for gameplay:

  • Game Functionality: Generating Sudoku puzzles, checking solution validity, managing undo/redo stacks, and persisting saved games.
  • Haptic Feedback: Providing subtle tactile vibrations when tapping cells or numbers (can be toggled in Settings).

7 Third-Party Service Providers

Skudo contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no telemetry/analytics services. The app operates completely stand-alone.

8 Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device in your private app sandbox.

  • Local Persistence: Data remains on your device for as long as you have the app installed.
  • Data Deletion: You have full control over your data. To permanently delete all saved games, stats, and preferences, simply clear the app's storage in your Android device settings (Settings > Apps > Skudo > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data) or uninstall the application.

9 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Skudo is a family-safe logic puzzle game. Because Skudo does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K.

10 Security Standards

Because Skudo does not transmit personal data across the internet or operate external databases, your information is protected by Android's built-in application sandboxing security model.
