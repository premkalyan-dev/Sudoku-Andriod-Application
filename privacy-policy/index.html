1 Overview & Developer Identity

Welcome to Skudo, a modern, feature-rich Sudoku puzzle game application created by Prem Kalyan ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy applies to the Android application Skudo, registered under the application package ID com.prem.skudo on Google Play.

We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data integrity, transparency, and user privacy in compliance with:

  • Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement and User Data Policies (including Data Safety requirements and Target SDK standards).
  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom.

2 Quick Summary (TL;DR)

ℹ️
Core Privacy Highlights:
  • You can play offline: Skudo does not require an account to enjoy basic Sudoku gameplay. Offline play stores puzzles locally on your device.

3 Information We Collect

Depending on whether you play as a guest or sign in with an account, we collect the following categories of data:

A. Account Information (Optional via Google Sign-In)

If you choose to sign in to sync your progress across devices, we use Firebase Authentication and Google Identity Services. We collect:

  • Display Name: To personalize your user profile and leaderboard ranking.
  • Email Address: Associated with your Google account for authentication and account identity.
  • Google User ID: A unique cryptographic identifier supplied by Google to associate your game data.
  • Profile Photo URL: (Optional) Displayed in your in-game profile header if provided by Google.

B. Gameplay & Progress Data (Cloud Firestore)

When signed in, your game progression is synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore to prevent progress loss:

  • Puzzles & Statistics: Puzzles completed, win streaks, best solve times, mistakes made, and difficulty level records.
  • Player Progression: XP (experience points), player level, earned rank, in-game coins, and gems.
  • Inventory & Purchases: Unlocked game themes, available hints, and shop inventory items.
  • Saved Game State: Board layout, pencil notes, and elapsed timer of an in-progress game so you can resume anytime.

C. Device & Technical Information

Standard diagnostics required by the Android operating system and Google Play Services:

  • Android OS version, device model, app version (e.g., Version 3.0, API Level 36), and system language for proper localization and rendering.

4 How We Use Your Information

We process your data solely for legitimate, user-facing operational purposes:

  • Game Functionality: Generating Sudoku boards, validating inputs, tracking hints, calculating mistake limits, and persisting saved games.
  • Cross-Device Synchronization: Backing up your achievements, gems, unlocked themes, and stats to your linked Google account so you never lose progress.
  • Authentication & Security: Verifying your identity through Google Sign-In, protecting against fraudulent account actions, and maintaining database integrity.
  • Haptic Feedback: Enhancing the interactive gameplay feel (vibrations on cell taps or mistakes) via device hardware.

6 Device Permissions

Skudo adheres to the principle of least privilege. The app requests only the minimum permissions necessary for gameplay:

Permission Level Purpose & Usage
android.permission.VIBRATE Normal Used strictly to provide haptic feedback when you tap numbers, toggle notes, or enter a number. You can toggle haptics on or off in the game settings.
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Required to securely communicate with Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud Firestore for profile sync.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Detects network availability so the app can gracefully queue updates locally when playing offline.
🔒
No Sensitive Permissions: Skudo does NOT access your location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, or external storage files.

8 Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide you with the game service:

  • Local Data: Stored in your device's local storage (Room Database and DataStore). You can clear this anytime by clearing the app's cache/storage or uninstalling the app.
  • Cloud Data: Retained in Firebase Firestore as long as your account is active. If your account is inactive for more than 24 months, or upon receiving your deletion request, your profile and game records are permanently deleted.

9 Account & Data Deletion Policy

In full accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion and Data Safety Requirements, any user who creates an account via Google Sign-In has the right to request the permanent deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

⚠️
What happens when you delete your account:
  • Your Firebase Authentication record is deleted.
  • Your Cloud Firestore user profile, stats, level, XP, coins, and gems are permanently purged.
  • All unlocked themes and hint inventories will be revoked and cannot be recovered.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion

You may initiate deletion via either of the following methods:

  1. In-App Deletion: Navigate to the in-game Settings/Profile menu in Skudo, tap "Sign Out & Clear Cloud Data".
  2. Web-Based Request (Google Play Mandated): Use the deletion form below or email us directly at premkalyan2727@gmail.com with the subject Skudo Data Deletion Request.

10 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Skudo is a general audience logic puzzle game designed for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union) without verifiable parental consent.

If you are a parent or guardian and become aware that your child has provided us with personal information via Google Sign-In without your consent, please contact us immediately at premkalyan2727@gmail.com. Upon verification, we will promptly delete such information from our servers.

11 Security & Encryption Standards

We implement rigorous technical and organizational security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All network communication between the Skudo app and Firebase/Google Cloud is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS) protocols.
  • Database Access Rules: Firebase Firestore security rules restrict database access so that each player can only view and update their own authorized player profile.
  • No Plaintext Passwords: We do not manage, store, or have access to passwords; authentication is delegated entirely to Google Identity Services.

12 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under applicable global privacy laws, you possess the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access / Know: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

  • Right to Data Portability: Obtain your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you (e.g., by denying gameplay) for exercising your privacy rights.
